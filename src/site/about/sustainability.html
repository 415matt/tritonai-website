Sustainable AI at UC San Diego

  1. TritonAI
  2. About
  3. Sustainable AI

Driving Innovation Responsibly

TritonAI uses both approved enterprise cloud services and open models hosted on UC-controlled infrastructure, including resources at the San Diego Supercomputer Center. The route depends on the service, data, required capability, and approved controls.

Responsible AI includes the resources used to build, run, and support a service. TritonAI aims to match the model and workflow to the task, avoid unnecessary processing, make costs visible, and reuse shared capabilities where practical.

UC-Hosted Infrastructure

SDSC supports energy-conscious computing through facility design, cooling, storage, and campus infrastructure. These features are relevant when an approved service uses a UC-hosted model, but they do not establish that every TritonAI query uses less energy or produces fewer emissions than every cloud alternative.

  • Optimized building orientation, sun shading, and low emissivity glass reduce heat gain and cooling demands.
  • CoolFlex rack systems keep hot and cold air separate, improving cooling efficiency and reducing fan energy.
  • A solar reflecting roof and high efficiency air handling units further decrease energy use. Sensors throughout the building track energy performance and guide future improvements.

SDSC also supports research into cleaner energy technologies, including wave-energy and battery research, reinforcing UC San Diego’s broader sustainability goals.

What This Means for TritonAI Teams

When designing or selecting an AI service:

  • Use the smallest capable model and avoid repeated or unnecessary work.
  • Evaluate quality, latency, cost, and resource use together.
  • Prefer shared knowledge, integrations, and services over duplicating infrastructure.
  • Measure environmental comparisons before making a public superiority claim.
  • Revisit the route as models, workloads, and evidence change.
