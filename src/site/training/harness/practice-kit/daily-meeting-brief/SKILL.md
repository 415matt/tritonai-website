# Daily meeting brief

Turn today's calendar into a brief someone can act on in five minutes.

## Gather (read-only)

Use the Microsoft 365 plugin. Only read; never send, accept, or change anything.

1. **Today's meetings.** Title, time (Pacific, 12-hour), organizer, attendees, location or link, and the invitation body.
2. **Last time.** For each recurring or related meeting, find the previous occurrence and its follow-up: notes, the follow-up email, or a Teams chat within a day of it. Pull the actions, owners, and decisions.
3. **Since then.** Mail and Teams chats from the past 7 days that mention the meeting's topic or come from its attendees.
4. **Attachments and links** in the invitation or those threads (agendas, decks, sheets). Open the ones the Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 plugins can read.

If something can't be found, say so. Never invent an action item, owner, date, or decision.

## Write the brief

### 1. Day at a glance (first)
- **Themes:** two or three threads that run across today's meetings.
- **Open loops:** actions that are overdue or due today, with owner.
- **Heads-up:** conflicts, back-to-back blocks, meetings with no agenda, anything that needs a decision.
- **Prep before 9:** the two or three things worth doing first.

### 2. Each meeting, in time order

**[Time] · [Meeting title]** · [who's attending, and their role if known]

- **Context:** why this meeting is happening and where things stand, in two or three sentences.
- **Carried over from last time:** each open action with owner and status (done, in progress, not started, unknown), and any decision still pending.
- **Suggested agenda:** three to five timed items that fit the meeting length, starting with carried-over items.
- **Best outcome:** one sentence on what "this went well" looks like.
- **Prep:** anything to read or bring, with links.
- **Sources:** the emails, chats, and files used, so the reader can check them.

## Rules

- Times in Pacific, 12-hour format.
- Mark anything inferred as "(inferred)".
- Keep each meeting under 150 words. The day at a glance goes first.
- Private or declined meetings: list the time only.
- Do not include sensitive details (health, HR cases, student records) from mail in the brief; say "sensitive thread, open it directly."
