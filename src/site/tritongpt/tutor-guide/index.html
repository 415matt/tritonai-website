TritonAI Tutor Guide

How to set up and tune the TritonAI Tutor in your Canvas course.

You control two things about your tutor: its knowledge base, the course materials it can retrieve from, and its system prompt, the written instructions it follows in every conversation with a student. These guides cover both.

To get to your tutor, open your Canvas course and click TritonAI Tutor in the course navigation on the left side of the page.
